Background

"I build systems that don't just output an answer — they show their work."

I'm an M.Tech student in Systems & Control Engineering at IIT Bombay, working at the intersection of computer vision, generative AI, and multi-agent systems. My thesis teaches machines to look at a satellite's components and explain what they see — pairing detection models with language models so the reasoning stays visible, not just the output.

Before IIT Bombay, I graduated top of my cohort in Computer Science & Engineering at Katihar Engineering College. Since then I've shipped a production RAG pipeline for a hiring startup, trained a language model to command a drone swarm, and co-authored work that helped bring a machine-learning framework into cancer research.

M.Tech thesis & seminar

Two years of research, in order

From a benchmarked pose-estimation seminar to an ongoing thesis on explainable satellite understanding.

Jul '26 — present
Explainable vision-language framework for space situational awareness
M.Tech thesis, advised by Prof. Srikanth Sukumar, Systems & Control Engineering
For my thesis, I'm building a system that can look at a satellite and identify its components with visual evidence attached, not just a label. That started with the data: a 5,000+ image dataset with full JSON/CSV metadata, bounding boxes, and YOLO annotations, backed by an automated auditing pipeline for validation, augmentation, class mapping, and grouped 5-fold cross-validation. On top of that, I've built YOLO26-S and YOLO26-S+ViT pipelines with conditional RT-DETR-L evaluation, benchmarked with GPU-accelerated mAP, precision, recall, F1, and IoU.
Future work
  • Extend the framework to 10+ satellite types and variants for cross-platform component detection and reasoning.
  • Integrate RAG and LLM reasoning for grounded component interpretation, anomaly analysis, and reporting.
Jan '26 — Apr '26
Markerless 6-DoF pose estimation with a hybrid CNN-transformer
M.Tech seminar, advised by Prof. Srikanth Sukumar, Systems & Control Engineering
Before the thesis, my seminar tackled a narrower but harder problem: estimating an object's full 6-DoF pose without markers, from a single image. I benchmarked 9 architectures across 5-fold cross-validation, about 45 full training runs, on 5,000+ synthetic images, then built a hybrid CNN-transformer estimator that cut MSE from 0.83 to 0.43 (a 48% reduction) and rotation error by 43%. It shipped as a live demo through Streamlit, GitHub, and Render CI/CD.
